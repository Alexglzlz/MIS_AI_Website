The Impact of the Mafiaboy cyberattacks

On February 2000, a 15-year-old hacker known as Mafiaboy launched a series of cyberattacks that disrupted major websites, including Yahoo!, eBay, and CNN. These attacks highlighted the vulnerabilities of online systems and the potential for significant economic and social disruption. The incident prompted increased awareness of cybersecurity threats and led to the implementation of stronger security measures across the internet.
