Correct AI Usage

I believe that AI should be used as a tool that can help us learn and understand things better than anything. The best use of AI would be to use it as a template or as a guide in your learning process. It should be used to help you understand things when you are confused and it should also be used to help you stand out any mistakes you might have made. It should basically only be double-checking your work instead of doing it for you.

AI Evaluation & critique

Your reasoning is strong and aligns well with academic integrity standards, as using AI as a personal guide and error-checker encourages active learning while preventing over-reliance. However, relying on AI strictly as a double-checker at the end of a project may slightly underutilize its capabilities. An alternative perspective suggests that AI can also serve as an effective co-creator during early brainstorming stages to help discover new viewpoints or outline complex ideas. As modern industries demand high AI literacy, learning how to actively collaborate with technology—rather than solely using it for final revisions—is an essential skill for future professionals.


Benefits of AI

  1. AI can help businesses make better decisions by analyzing large amounts of data quickly and accurately.
  2. AI can automate repetitive tasks, freeing up employees to focus on more complex and creative work.
  3. AI can improve customer experiences by providing personalized recommendations and support.

Who Benefits?

  1. Businesses can benefit from increased efficiency and productivity, as well as improved decision-making capabilities.
  2. Consumers can benefit from personalized experiences and improved products and services.
  3. Society as a whole can benefit from the potential for AI to address complex problems and improve quality of life.

How AI Tools Used

  • AI tools can be used to analyze data and identify patterns that humans may not be able to detect.
  • AI tools can be used to automate tasks such as customer service, data entry, and inventory management.
  • AI tools can be used to develop new products and services, such as personalized recommendations and predictive analytics.


Incorrect AI Usage



AI Misuse in Education
AI can be misused in education by providing students with answers to assignments or exams, which can lead to academic dishonesty and a lack of learning.
AI Misuse in Exams
AI can be misused in exams by providing students with answers, which can lead to academic dishonesty.
AI Misuse in Interview Process
AI can be misused in the interview process by providing biased or inaccurate candidate evaluations, which can lead to unfair hiring decisions.

In my opinion, I think AI is a great tool that can facilitate the learning procress. There are so many incredible uses of AI that can help those who might struggle to understand simple things. However, I do think that AI can be misused incredibly. Many use AI to do all their work and gain no new undertandings from anything their are being taught.
